97CLIPZ

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

97CLIPZ ("the App") is a personal tool, run by its owner, that uploads short video clips the owner has created to the owner's own TikTok account. This policy explains what information the App uses and how.

Who uses the App

The App is not a public service. It is used only by its owner to manage their own TikTok account. No one else can sign up for or use it.

Information the App accesses

When the owner connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives only what TikTok provides for the permissions granted:

  • Basic account information, such as the account's open ID, display name and avatar, used to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used to upload videos to that account.

The App does not access followers, messages, likes, viewing history, contacts, or any other TikTok user's data.

How information is used

The information above is used only to authenticate with TikTok and upload videos to the owner's account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Storage and security

Access tokens and video files are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server other than TikTok's, and are not shared with anyone.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, as needed to upload videos.

Cookies and tracking

The App and this website do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the TikTok account remains connected. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
